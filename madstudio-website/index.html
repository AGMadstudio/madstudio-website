How a project runs

One studio from the first meeting to handover, with a fixed scope and price agreed before work starts.

  1. Consultation

    We meet on site or at the studio, walk the space and understand how you live.

  2. Concept

    Layouts, mood and 3D visuals so you can see the space before anything is built.

  3. Design & pricing

    Detailed drawings, materials and a full bill of quantities with a fixed price.

  4. Approvals & build

    We handle the NOCs and permits, then our team builds on a clear schedule.

  5. Handover

    A final walkthrough, snagging closed out, and the keys back to you.
